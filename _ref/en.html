DJ based in Curitiba, Brazil,

Shaping the sound identity of clubs such as Warung, Club Vibe and Terraza.

With more than 20 years on the dance floor, Aninha was a long-time resident at Warung Beach Club and Club Vibe, and has been part of the D-Edge and Surreal Park ecosystem since 2019.

Aninha is a South American reference in warm-up sets: a digger, a pioneer and a specialist in building atmospheres that move from house to techno with precision.
